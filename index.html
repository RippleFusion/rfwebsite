How every engagement typically runs

From the first week to the last.

  1. 1Start from the facts

    The current state assessed on arrival, whether it is a contract, an operating model, a cost base or a programme. The gap to the target set out in plain terms.

  2. 2Agree the target and the route

    What good looks like, who owns what and by when, agreed with the CEO, the board and the sponsor.

  3. 3Deliver with the business

    I work inside your team and your governance: weekly updates to senior leadership and a monthly board pack, until the benefits land.

  4. 4Hand over and leave

    The right skills, partners and permanent leadership in place. I leave when the business runs it without me.

Where an engagement needs extra depth, I bring in senior specialists I have delivered with before.
