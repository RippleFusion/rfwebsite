The Problem

Everyone can see the potential. Few can turn it into a plan

Most leadership teams already know AI, automation and digital transformation matter. What’s missing isn’t ambition, it’s practical guidance: where to focus first, what to prioritise, and how to translate emerging technology into real business value, in language that survives contact with a board.

That gap is usually filled one of two ways: an expensive strategy deck with no delivery muscle behind it, or a technical hire who can build but can’t yet be trusted with the room. Both leave leaders exposed at exactly the moment they need clarity most.

How I Work

Three principles behind every piece of work I take on

Business First

Where every engagement starts

Complex business problems diagnosed at the root, then solved with technology only where it earns its place. The right answer is often a smarter process, not a new platform.

Built For Delivery

How the work actually gets done

Delivery focused from the first day. Plans become programmes, teams are mobilised around outcomes, and momentum is built early instead of leaving a strategy deck on the shelf.

Operating Model Redesign

What makes the change last

Strategy, operating model, governance and organisation redesigned together, with management consulting rigour and a practical approach that makes change stick across the business.

How I Engage

Choose the level of involvement you need

Interim Programme Lead

For organisations launching a transformation programme that needs a hands-on leader from day one

Hands-on leadership of your transformation from first assessment through delivery, adoption and benefits realisation, with the governance and delivery discipline to scale it properly.

Rescue Project Specialist

For programmes that have gone wrong and need an experienced leader to take control

Stepping in when an ERP, CRM or other big-ticket transformation is struggling. Diagnosing what has gone wrong, shoring up governance and delivery, and getting the programme back on track.

Embedded Strategist

For teams already moving who need sharper prioritisation and ROI discipline

Working alongside your leadership and technical teams to find the right opportunities, build the business case and keep transformation initiatives aligned to outcomes, not just activity.

Interim Chief Data & AI Officer

For organisations that need senior data and AI ownership now, not later

Full ownership of the data and AI agenda alongside the wider programme, covering strategy, governance, quality and delivery, until a permanent leader is in place.

Advisory

For leadership teams who need direction, not headcount

Strategic advice at board or executive level on where transformation will create the most value, and how AI fits into it. Assessing the opportunity, setting priorities and building the roadmap, so you have credible thinking before you commit resources.

Non-Executive Director (NED)

For boards that want experienced transformation and technology expertise at the table

An experienced voice at board level, challenging strategy, assessing risk and keeping transformation, data and AI decisions grounded in commercial reality.
