The work I take on

Six situations where I'm brought in.

  • Separation and transition

    A business has to leave its parent or its incumbent service provider.

    • Transition services agreement negotiated alongside legal counsel
    • Day 1 planned across finance, HR, IT, suppliers and contracts
    • Stranded and double running costs sized, tracked and negotiated down

    Done beforeA two year transition across 180+ markets, exited on the contractual date.

  • Target operating model

    Every region or acquired business works a different way.

    • Current state assessed, the gap set out, one model designed for every region
    • Sales, operations and finance on common processes and platforms
    • Shared services built onshore and offshore, with clear accountability

    Done beforeOperating costs down 25% across four regions, with service quality held.

  • Acquisition integration

    A serial acquirer needs every deal integrated the same way, at pace.

    • An acquisition production line, not a one off project each time
    • Day 1 planned for each business: people, process, technology and finance
    • Board reporting on operations and finance from one version of the truth

    Done before9 businesses across 5 countries integrated at 2 a month.

  • Programme turnaround

    A critical programme is late, over budget or has lost the board's confidence.

    • Fast diagnosis: risks, dependencies and what to stop
    • Governance reset, with failing teams and partners replaced
    • Stabilised, delivered and handed over able to run without me

    Done beforeA failing $47m finance programme turned around and delivered for 20% less.

  • Cost, sourcing and global delivery

    The cost base is too high and work sits in the wrong places.

    • Partner selection from RFI to contract, with terms that pay for outcomes, not effort
    • Work moved offshore and nearshore, with knowledge transfer managed
    • Vendors consolidated and savings tracked by the business year after year

    Done before$130m+ of annual savings across 180+ markets.

  • Exit readiness and transaction support

    A sale or IPO is on the horizon.

    • The change programme aligned to the sale timetable
    • Due diligence: buyer information prepared and questions answered
    • The programme presented to buyers and advisers

    Done beforeProgramme and due diligence support behind a $6.5bn sale.

How an engagement runs

Built to exit.

  1. 1Diagnose

    The current state across people, process and technology, assessed on arrival. The gap to the target is set out in plain terms.

  2. 2Plan

    One roadmap with governance, risks, dependencies and the critical path, and a budget with forecast and variance.

  3. 3Deliver

    Weekly updates to the senior leadership, a monthly board pack and a seat on the steering committees until the benefits land.

  4. 4Hand over and leave

    The right skills, partners and permanent leadership in place. I leave when the business runs it without me.

Where a programme needs extra depth, I bring in senior specialists I have delivered with before.
